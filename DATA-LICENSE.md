# Data and provenance

The original code, manually authored decision descriptions, catalog structure, synthetic evaluation fixtures, original benchmark annotations and reports in this repository are MIT licensed. They use factual model identifiers, account numbers and official labels drawn from the linked references. Each catalog record carries its sources and each catalog has a review/version date.

Official source documents are not republished under this project's MIT license. The optional public PDF evaluations download material from AEAT, BOE, other Spanish tax administrations and the IRS into the ignored `eval/corpus/` directory. The [frozen public corpus manifest](eval/public-v1.json) records source URLs, file and input hashes, selected physical pages and original annotations. Published results contain predictions, metrics and provenance, not PDF files or extracted source text. Consult the source sites' terms for reuse beyond this project; public availability is not a new license. Synthetic examples are invented and are not genuine returns or filing receipts.

The upstream `kyotofin/tax-doc-classifier` is Apache-2.0 and is acknowledged as architectural inspiration. This project does not contain upstream source files, IRS criteria or benchmark data, and does not relicense them. Any future contribution copying third-party implementation must preserve its applicable license and notices.

The optional `@llamaindex/liteparse` engine is Apache-2.0 and retains its own package license. Our adapter is original MIT code; no DocJev source is copied. The [fresh Spanish sample manifest](eval/calibration-v1.json) likewise references source-owned PDFs rather than distributing them. University accounting material linked in the research notes is only a candidate reference; its noncommercial/share-alike terms are not converted to MIT, and its content is not bundled.
